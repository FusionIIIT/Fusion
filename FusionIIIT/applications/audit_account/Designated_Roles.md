# Module Name: Audit Account

## Designated User Roles & Permissions

### 1. Role Name: Director
* **Description:** Primary authority for the audit account module, overseeing all institutional budgets.
* **Permissions:**
    * View high-level summaries and dashboards of all department budgets.
    * Final approval of critical budget adjustments and major anomalies.
    * Access to comprehensive audit logs and reporting tools.

### 2. Role Name: Auditor
* **Description:** Financial auditor responsible for reviewing and verifying account records.
* **Permissions:**
    * Read access to all financial transaction records and department requests.
    * Flag anomalies or discrepancies in account statements.
    * Generate detailed audit reports for the Director and relevant HODs.

### 3. Role Name: Head of Department (HOD)
* **Description:** Manager of a specific department's budget and requests.
* **Permissions:**
    * View and manage the allocated budget for their specific department.
    * Create, update, and submit budget requests to the Accountant/Auditor.
    * View the status of their department's submitted requests.

### 4. Role Name: Accountant
* **Description:** Standard operational user responsible for daily financial data entry and processing.
* **Permissions:**
    * Process approved budget requests.
    * Edit specific financial fields (e.g., status updates, budget allocations).
    * Maintain and update the daily ledger and transaction records.

### 5. Role Name: Employee
* **Description:** End-user submitting individual financial requests (e.g., reimbursements, petty cash).
* **Permissions:**
    * Read-only access to their personal request records.
    * Ability to submit individual financial requests via the frontend.
